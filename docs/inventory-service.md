# Inventory service

## Purpose and implemented behavior

Keep inventory accounting and service-trip policy outside branch, stairs, and vein pattern execution. At each existing pressure checkpoint the service best-effort consolidates compatible stacks, then compares occupied slots to `inventory.returnThreshold` (default 14). The coordinator checks around floor-main cells, during both branch phases, and after each completed stair slice. Vein traversal checks pressure before every candidate dig; when pressure is reached, it unwinds before another discovery and the caller services only at the exact tunnel checkpoint. A reached threshold persists the exact mining pose/cursor before returning over checked known-clear edges. Before each travel leg it saves the expected destination pose; after arrival it records the actual base or service checkpoint. It records the unloading boundary before routing items and after routing completes, then the departure boundary before replaying the saved pose. The mining pose/cursor remains in the service record throughout. It clears the service record only after restoring the saved pose. A failed checkpoint write stops before its associated leg or stage, and a failed durable clear is returned as a service failure. Low torch stock can also force the same service path before the inventory threshold; status text names that trigger separately so it is not mistaken for a full inventory.

When the coordinator supplies a resupply callback, base service re-verifies the left chest after unloading, restores fuel to the computed resume-and-return commitment plus reserve, fills the configured torch target, checks both before departure, and restores canonical facing. Fuel/torch pulls are bounded and support either chest ordering. Temporary `NO_FUEL_SUPPLY` and `NO_TORCHES` outcomes keep the service checkpoint at base; the coordinator retries every five seconds and resumes the exact saved route when supplies arrive. Those service errors remain recoverable after reboot through `miner resume`. Missing chests, unsupported items, and transfer faults stop for operator repair. When `base.separateBulk` is enabled, every service trip also verifies the configured rear chest from the surface pose before writing a departure checkpoint or moving. A missing or mismatched rear chest returns `NO_BULK_CHEST` at the mining checkpoint. Before an exposed ore seed is dug in any of the forward, up, or down scan directions, the coordinator checks consolidated inventory pressure and completes the normal checkpointed service trip if the threshold has been reached. The seed is entered only after service returns to its exact scan pose.

## Public entry points

- `require("src.inventory.service").new(dependencies)` creates a service with `pressureReached()`, `serviceIfNeeded()`, and `resume(savedTarget)` operations. `resume` replays a classified interrupted trip while retaining the original mining target. Deferred `checkpoint`, `progress`, `route`, and `configSnapshot` dependencies enable durable service stage tracking.
- `require("src.inventory.resupply").run(options)` classifies bounded left-chest pulls, verifies refuel deltas and torch totals, and restores the selected slot.
- Dependencies are injected: turtle API, runtime/item configuration, pose getter, facing/movement callbacks, and report callbacks. The module keeps no inventory or pose copy.
- `require("src.inventory.pressure").slotCounts(turtleApi)` returns occupied and free slot counts; `occupiedSlots` and `reached` expose the related pressure queries.
- `require("src.inventory.pressure").consolidate(turtleApi)` makes a best-effort pass merging compatible same-name stacks.
- `require("src.inventory.pressure_gate").beforeDig(pressureReached, serviceIfNeeded)` admits a dig only after a clear pressure probe or successful inventory service.
- Accounting remains in `src.inventory.pressure` and `src.inventory.chest`; selected-slot preservation uses `src.inventory.slot_guard`.

## Invariants and assumptions

- Pressure uses `inventory.returnThreshold`; configuration validation accepts integer values from 1 through 15. `inventory.autoConsolidate` defaults to true; setting it to false skips consolidation. Service reports the measured occupied-slot count and configured threshold when pressure triggers, and reports low torch stock separately.
- Vein seed admission is evaluated before saving an `entering` vein record and before the seed dig. A failed service trip returns a typed inventory-service error without digging or moving into the seed.
- Consolidation considers matching item names only as candidates. It trusts `transferTo` only when source decrease and target increase match, compacts later slots into earlier slots without reversing progress, and leaves incompatible NBT variants untouched.
- Consolidation is best-effort; incompatible or full target pairs may remain split. It restores the previously selected slot and reports verified total transferred.
- An unload trip returns through the existing cleared route and restores the saved coordinates and facing only through checked coordinator movement callbacks. From an exact recorded floor landing, the coordinator uses each saved stair route in reverse to surface and replays the recorded stair centreline on departure, then follows known-clear edges back to the exact landing checkpoint. Other phase checkpoints use only the known-route graph for both legs; no shortcut or inferred coordinate is used.
- Startup `resume` replays validated service stages only when their complete mining cursor is dispatchable and known routes connect the committed pose, base, and saved target. Known-pose `NO_SUPPLY_CHEST`, `NO_FUEL_SUPPLY`, and `NO_TORCHES` service failures preserve their stage and can be retried after the operator fixes the chest layout or restocks it; resume repeats chest validation, unload/resupply, and clears service only after exact target restoration. Other fatal errors are not made retryable by this exception. It also replays a durable `floor_complete` checkpoint through recorded edges and stair centreline. Pending actions, veins, and unsupported stair cursors remain fail closed.
- Chest acceptance uses exact configured block IDs. Missing sides return `NO_SUPPLY_CHEST`, `NO_OUTPUT_CHEST`, or `NO_BULK_CHEST`. Partial acceptance returns `CHEST_FULL`; other drop faults return `UNLOAD_FAILED`. Bulk-separated service requires the rear chest at pre-departure admission and verifies it again at unloading.
- Configured bulk IDs route rear only when `base.separateBulk=true`. All remaining excess, including ores and unknown items, routes right.
- `inventory.retainedItems["minecraft:cobblestone"]` protects the configurable mandatory main-shaft backfill reserve across arbitrary slots, regardless of paving state. Unloading drops only cobblestone above this reserve.
- The slot guard restores the selected slot after unloading, including helper failures.
- The service is single-turtle and synchronous; the coordinator owns live pose and persistent checkpoints.

## Dependencies and limitations

Depends on the injected CC:Tweaked turtle API and the pressure, chest, slot-guard, checkpoint, and resupply modules. Before replay, the coordinator budgets recorded travel from the committed pose to base and the checkpoint-to-base-to-checkpoint service commitment. Live chest ordering/content, stair service from non-landing checkpoints, and multi-trip verification remain in-world checks.

## Verification

`tests/inventory_service.lua` covers destination-pose snapshots at every trip boundary, failed pre-departure checkpoint rejection, rear-chest admission before service travel, base resupply ordering, failed service-record clear, interrupted-service replay to the exact original target, and a rejected full-output drop that leaves inventory at base without replaying the mining route. `tests/inventory_pressure_gate.lua` verifies clear-pressure admission, service-before-dig at the threshold, fail-closed service errors, and that non-true pressure results do not trigger service. `tests/service_route.lua` models a recorded route through surface entry, eight stair slices, a landing, and a branch checkpoint; it verifies exact return to origin and exact checkpoint/facing restoration without unrecorded edges. `tests/inventory_resupply.lua` covers both fuel/torch chest orders, verified fuel/torch targets, selected-slot restoration, and absent fuel. `tests/stair_route.lua` verifies the post-slice service hook and stop-on-service-failure behavior; `tests/active_baseline_wiring.lua` checks main and both branch phases; `tests/vein_traversal.lua` verifies pressure forces exact checkpoint unwind before service. No in-world verification was performed; full-chest behavior in the actual gallery remains a release check.
